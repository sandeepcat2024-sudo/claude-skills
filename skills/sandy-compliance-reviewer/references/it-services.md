# IT services — specific checks
- **Contracts:** MSA + SOW with scope, change requests, acceptance criteria, timelines, payment milestones, IP transfer on payment, warranties, liability cap, confidentiality, non-solicit.
- **Partner/white-label:** back-to-back contract with delivery partner (same scope, SLAs, IP, confidentiality); disclose subcontracting if the client contract requires it.
- **Case studies/logos:** only with written permission; label partner's work honestly (no claiming it as his own team's work without truth).
- **Data and security:** client data access → data-processing terms, DPDP/GDPR, secure credentials handling, no personal devices without policy.
- **Staff augmentation:** worker classification, contracts, and payment compliance for freelancers.
- **Tax:** GST on services; export of services → LUT; TDS on payments to freelancers where applicable. Flag for a CA.
