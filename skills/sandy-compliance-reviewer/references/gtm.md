# GTM consulting — specific checks
- **Claims:** past results must be his own, anonymised, and not reveal ex-employer confidential data. "Helped grow revenue X%" needs to be true and defensible.
- **Proposals/contracts:** clear scope, deliverables, fees, payment terms, IP ownership, confidentiality, limitation of liability, termination. Recommend a lawyer-reviewed template once.
- **Client data:** if he touches client CRM/lead data, he is likely a data processor — needs a data-processing clause and DPDP/GDPR care.
- **Outbound for clients:** client's email domains, opt-outs, sending limits; never buy scraped lists that break law or platform terms.
- **Social proof:** no fake followers, engagement pods that breach terms, or AI-made testimonials.
- **Tax:** professional services → GST threshold for services; export of services (foreign clients) → LUT; possible TDS by Indian clients. Flag for a CA.
