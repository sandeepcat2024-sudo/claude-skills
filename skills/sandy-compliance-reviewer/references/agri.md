# Agricultural products — specific checks (verify current rules before acting)
- **Food safety:** any food business (including trading/packing) needs FSSAI registration or licence depending on turnover and activity.
- **Labels (packaged):** FSSAI labelling rules + Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules — name, net quantity, MRP, manufacturer/packer address, dates, FSSAI number, veg mark, allergens.
- **"Organic" claims:** only with valid certification (NPOP / PGS-India). Otherwise ⛔.
- **Health claims:** no unapproved medicinal or health claims ("cures", "boosts immunity") — FSSAI and ASCI.
- **Agri inputs:** seeds, fertilisers and pesticides need dealer licences under their specific Acts/Orders.
- **Trade:** state APMC/mandi rules for buying from farmers; fair, written terms with farmers/FPOs; timely payment.
- **Export:** IEC from DGFT; RCMC from APEDA (or relevant board, e.g. Spices Board for spices); phytosanitary certificate; importing country's rules (e.g. pesticide residue limits).
- **Tax:** GST rates differ (many unprocessed farm products exempt; branded/packaged items may be taxable); e-way bills for goods movement. Flag for a CA.
