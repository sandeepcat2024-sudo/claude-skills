# Children's ebooks (Amazon KDP) — specific checks
- **KDP content guidelines:** no misleading metadata (titles, keywords naming other authors/brands), no content copying, correct age category.
- **AI disclosure:** KDP asks publishers to disclose AI-generated text/images at publishing. Answer truthfully.
- **IP:** no known characters (e.g. famous cartoon versions of deities or brands), no copyrighted art styles copied closely, licensed fonts only. Traditional mythology stories are fine to retell in original words.
- **Cultural and religious sensitivity:** respectful depiction of deities, festivals and communities; check facts about rituals; avoid stereotypes. Have a parent from the target audience read it if possible.
- **Children's safety:** age-appropriate content; marketing aimed at parents, not children; no collecting children's data (COPPA in the US, DPDP Act protections for children in India).
- **Reviews:** never buy, swap or incentivise reviews (Amazon policy).
- **Tax:** KDP tax interview (W-8BEN for Indian residents to claim treaty rate); royalties are taxable income in India. Flag for a CA.
