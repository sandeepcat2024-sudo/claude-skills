## Business-specific playbook
- **Delivery first:** without a reliable delivery partner, every sale is a risk. Shortlist and vet 2–3 partners (portfolio, references, sample task) before outbound.
- **Niche down:** one service × one industry × one geography beats "we do everything".
- **Leads:** Apollo/lead-finder for ICP lists; job postings as buying signals (company hiring for a role you can outsource).
- **Sales assets:** capability deck, 2–3 case studies (partner's, with permission and clearly labelled), SOW template, pricing calculator.
- **KPIs:** meetings booked, proposal win rate, gross margin per project, partner on-time delivery.
